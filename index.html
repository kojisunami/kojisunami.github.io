Research

My interest is typically modern perspective of algebraic geometry to solve Fukaya category and non-linear PDE. Here is a list of research proposals:

    On Homological Mirror Symmetry

    For Proving HMS 1: Hochschild deformation of Fukaya category or Stable ∞, which induces derived formal geometry, 2: Comparing Fukaya category with P⁢e⁢r⁢f⁢(Y), whose Tannakian duality is Y. [link]

  • ♠

    Derived Intersection on Foliation

    For Proving HMS 3: If all symplectic are somewhat cotangent, Nadler-Zaslow might be generalized to any Fukaya category. [link]

  • ♠

    Classical Mechanics on Quantum Satake

    Quantum Satake (analogue of Fourier-Mukai everybody knows) of O⁢G⁢(5,10) is Apery’s diffyq, and compute classical mechanics from quantum approach in general. We consider NSE and LG model from SCFT, or SUGRA from superstring theory? [link]

  • ♠

    Categorification of Quiver

    R⁢e⁢p⁢(G) is symmetric monoidal abelian, while R⁢e⁢p⁢(Q) is abelian but not symmetric monoidal. Is there any categorification of Dynkin/non-Dynkin diagram? [link]

Casual Non-Research Interests

Below is the list of materials I personally think interesting, and they are somewhat connecting to algebraic geometry. They are quite different from my focus, however.

  • ♣

    AdS/CFT
    AdS/CFT claims AdS conformal boundary corresponds to CFT, called holography. AdS/CFT induces Ryu-Takayanagi’s quantum-entanglement, CFT generates a variety of non-linear PDE including relativistic Navier-Stokes equation, and Sasaki-Einstein models AdS space.

  • ♣

    Spin(7) Geometry and M-theory
    M-theory is a generalization of 5 superstring theories, while simplifies perturbation effect. M-theory consists of G2 holonomy in 11-dimensional space. Joyce conjecture claims an extraction of symplectic structure from G2.

  • ♣

    Geometric Langlands
    Geometric Langlands bridges local system, D-module, and Higgs bundle, and this is not number theory. 4d CFT derives Higgs bundle through Kapustin-Witten, whose S-duality is D-module.

  • ♣

    Vertex Operator Algebra
    There are many application in physics and other area of math.

He recommended me a book titled ”A Mathematician’s Survial Guide” https://www.amazon.com/Mathematicians-Survival-Guide-Graduate-Development/dp/082183455X This book tells us not only the academic system involving qualifying exams and thesis defense, but including PhD admission and postgraduation career path. I became interested in becoming PhD student partly because of this. There is also a free online link ”Postdoc Career Advice” https://math.ou.edu/ kmartin/career-ad.html.

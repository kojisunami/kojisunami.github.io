\item (PhD Admission)

\href{https://www.amazon.com/Mathematicians-Survival-Guide-Graduate-Development/dp/082183455X}
{https://www.amazon.com/Mathematicians-Survival-Guide-Graduate-Development/dp/082183455X},

I'd also recommend a free online link "Postdoc Career Advice" 
\href{https://math.ou.edu/~kmartin/career-ad.html}{https://math.ou.edu/~kmartin/career-ad.html}.

and I hope I have an outline of research before admission if we plan to graduate in 3 or 4 years.

\textbf{Philosophy} \\

\begin{itemize}
\item Good Philosophy \\
Darwin and UNIX and Newton are the best philosophy, since the question is clear, everybody can understand, 
and understanding will be deepened the more we study on it.
Meanwhile, statistics and quantum mechanics and Marxism are nobody understands, everybody can prentend to know.
I hope math will good side of philosophy.

\item Abstract Nonsense \\
Math is like climbing mountain. Skilled climber says climb up to the notch rather than climb down if we're lost(why).
Abstract nonsense is like the apex of the mountain, doesn't mean there is some meaning in itself.

\item Good Way of Reading \\
Good way of reading philosophy is how to read it not what exactly is written, since the author might be lying or mistaking.
Conversely, making a mistake isn't a catastrophic, since as long as we are a good thinker, we can debug error,
while lying is definitely not recommended, since a word is for representing an idea. 
Nevertheless, lying is still possible, then I recommended hacking contest.

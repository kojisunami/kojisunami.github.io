\textbf{Career As Mathematician}

\begin{itemize}
\item (Undergraduate) My personal undergraduate life especially after my third year is to study abstract algebra
with a view toward Hartshorne or representation theory, and as a consequence I have (probably) a strong recommendation letter.
This isn't probably a mistake at the moment, and the best possible thing that I could imagine is to continue studying
algebraic geometry and homological algebra and other abstract algebra stuff.
Meanwhile, one my regrets is that I haven't study much of geometry and analysis, 
but I personally think it's probably not the significant delay, even though PhD thesis would contain some of them,
since some of geometry and analysis often appear in Fukaya category argument,
and I should have read research works of professors of our research group.
Nevertheless, thorough research statement wasn't quite neccesary for the acceptance to the top 100 school.

\item (PhD and Postdoc) When I was undergradute, I noticed the existence of GTA job in US institute as PhD, 
by my professor's recommendation. He recommended me a book titled "A Mathematician’s Survial Guide” 
\href{https://www.amazon.com/Mathematicians-Survival-Guide-Graduate-Development/dp/082183455X}
{https://www.amazon.com/Mathematicians-Survival-Guide-Graduate-Development/dp/082183455X}
This book tells us not only the academic system involving qualifying exams and thesis defense, 
but including PhD admission and postgraduation career path. I became interested in becoming PhD student partly because of this.
There is also a free online link "Postdoc Career Advice" 
\href{https://math.ou.edu/~kmartin/career-ad.html}{https://math.ou.edu/~kmartin/career-ad.html}.

\item (Drop Out) On the other hand, 
in case we will drop out from PhD and in case we will not be able to find a job that is related to our specialization, 
there’s still tons of moderately paid good jobs like fisherman for hand-to-mouth.
Data Annotation \href{https://dataannotation.tech}{https://dataannotation.tech}
(there are many other good sites) introduces part-time employment on online platform, 
where its duty is typically judging and fixing the error of AI decision by human critical thinking. 
Data Annotation offers many data science and programming jobs, including training AI for legal and medical stuffs,
but in particular, it offers job for training AI for enhancing its math proof ability, or if you are bilingual, 
I’d recommend translation of technical terms on online platform as a part-time job. 
There’s tons of stuffs that AI cannot accurately translate, so pay is good.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Good Philosophy} \\

\begin{itemize}
\item Good Philosophy \\
Darwin and UNIX and Newton are the best philosophy in history, or it might be symmetry/structure equivalent in math.
Ideally, the question is clear, everybody can understand, nobody can imitate.
Meanwhile, statistics and quantum mechanics and Marxism are nobody understands, everybody can imitate.

\item Abstract Nonsense \\
Math is like climbing mountain. Skilled climber says climb up to the notch rather than climb down if we're lost(why).
Abstract nonsense is like the apex of the mountain, doesn't mean there is some meaning in itself.

\item Good Way of Reading \\
Good way of reading philosophy is how to read it not what exactly is written, 
since the author might be lying or mistaking, and math is the same.

\item Intro to Socrates \\
Socrates was the smartest person by the time of Oracle. If Masters' degree is literally mastering, it means smart,
and if PhD is about philosophy, it's something different.
\end{itemize}

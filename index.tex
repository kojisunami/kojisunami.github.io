\textbf{Casual Non-Research Interests} \\

\noindent
Below is the list of materials I personally think interesting, and they are somewhat connecting to algebraic geometry. 
They are quite different from my focus, however.

\newline

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{AdS/CFT} \\
AdS/CFT claims AdS conformal boundary corresponds to CFT, called holography.
AdS/CFT induces Ryu-Takayanagi's quantum-entanglement,
CFT generates a variety of non-linear PDE including relativistic Navier-Stokes equation, and Sasaki-Einstein models AdS space.

\item \textbf{Spin(7) Geometry and M-theory} \\
M-theory is a generalization of $5$ superstring theories, while simplifies perturbation effect.
M-theory consists of $G_2$ holonomy in $11$-dimensional space. Joyce conjecture claims an extraction of symplectic structure from $G_2$.

\item \textbf{Geometric Langlands} \\
Geometric Langlands bridges local system, D-module, and Higgs bundle, and this is not number theory.  
4d CFT derives Higgs bundle through Kapustin-Witten, whose S-duality is D-module.

\item \textbf{Vertex Operator Algebra} \\
There are many application in physics and other area of math.
\end{itemize}

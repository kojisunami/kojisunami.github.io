\begin{definition}{Geometric Prequantization}{}
Prequantization is a process of making an operator $Q(f)$ from a smooth function $f$, $Q: C^\infty(X) \to End(\mathcal{H})$
on a symplectic manifold $(X,\omega)$.
More precisely, $\mathcal{H} = \Gamma^\infty(L)$ where $L$ is a prequantum line bundle with a connection $\nabla_{X_f}$,
and an operator given by $Q(f) := -i\hbar \nabla_{X_f} + f$, so that bracket satisfies $[Q(f),Q(g)] = i\hbar Q(\{f,g\})$. \\
\end{definition}

\noindent
Typically, the operator is Kostant–Souriau operator $Q(f) := -i\hbar (X_f + \frac{1}{i\hbar}\theta(X_f)) + f$. \\



\begin{definition}{Deformation Quantization}{}
Deformation quantization transforms ordinary product to star product $fg\mapsto f\star g$ where the star product is defined by
\[f\star g = \Sigma_{n\geqq 0} \frac{1}{n!} (\frac{i\hbar}{2})^n \Pi^n (f,g)\]
where $\Pi$ is Poisson bivector, and $\Pi^1(f,g) = \{f,g\}$ is ordinary bracket. Naturally, the Poisson bracket is defined by
\[\frac{f\star g - g\star f}{i\hbar} = \{f,g\} + O(\hbar) \]
\end{definition}

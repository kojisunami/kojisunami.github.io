\textbf{Research} \\

\noindent
My current interest is homological mirror symmetry(HMS) from derived formal geometry and microlocal analysis.
In these notes below is some speculations and calculations. My research proposal is little broad, so I split it into two sections. 
\end{minipage}


\noindent
\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
\noindent $\spadesuit$ \textbf{On Homological Mirror Symmetry I} \\
Beginning from Nadler-Zaslow, I conjecture existence of methodology of generalization of higher Fukaya category of 
n-shifted cotangent bundle over some stack, whose derived Lagrangian intersection induces Fukaya category of any Calabi-Yau $X$,
and positive dimensionality of nc fp dg-manifold inside Fukaya category to discuss intersection theory in virtual language.
\href{https://kojisunami.github.io/src/ainfty-geometry.pdf}{[link]} 
\end{minipage}


\noindent
\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
\noindent $\spadesuit$ \textbf{On Homological Mirror Symmetry II} \\
I conjecture the generalization of sheaf quantization to Fukaya-Seidel category through Fedosov method of deformation quantization
or deformation quantization of matrix factorization, which induces Lie groupoid, controlling derived Lagrangian foliation,
while I also consider perverse schober interpretation of Fukaya-Seidel category, and Riemann-Hilbert correspondence of derived foliation.
\href{https://kojisunami.github.io/src/dgla-stack.pdf}{[link]}  
\end{minipage}

\noindent $\spadesuit$ \textbf{Navier-Stokes Equation on 4D Mirror Symmetry} \\
Converting Navier-Stokes Equation through AdS/CFT correspondence to 4d CFT, which induces connection form equation $\nabla f=0$.
This 4d CFT is a problem of 4d mirror symmetry or it reduces to Higgs bundle. 
I'll study these relationship, namely construction of 4d mirror symmetry from either Fukaya category or Nakajima quiver variety,
and I'd also consider Apery differential equation in quantum Satake counting quantum connection through its spectrum. 
\href{https://kojisunami.github.io/src/pde.pdf}{[link]}  
\end{minipage}

\noindent
\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
\noindent $\clubsuit$ \textbf{Spin(7) Geometry and M-theory} \\
M-theory is a generalization of $5$-superstring theories type I,IIA,IIB,SO(32),heterotic,E8xE8, while simplifies perturbation effect.
M-theory consists of $G_2$ holonomy in $11$-dimensional space. Joyce conjecture claims an extraction of symplectic structure from $G_2$,
by associative 3-form as an analogue of Calabi-Yau 3-fold. Typical strategy is deformation and wall-crossing formula.
What's more, ALE space and Fourier-Mukai.
\end{minipage}

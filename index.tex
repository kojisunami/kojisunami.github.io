\item \textbf{AdS/CFT} \\
AdS/CFT claims AdS conformal boundary corresponds to CFT, called holography.
AdS/CFT induces Ryu-Takayanagi's quantum-entanglement,
Sasaki-Einstein models AdS space, while CFT generates a variety of non-linear PDE including relativistic Navier-Stokes equation,
and vertex operator algebra if $N=2$, while 4d CFT derives Higgs bundle through Kapustin-Witten, whose S-duality is D-module.

\item \textbf{Spin(7) Geometry and M-theory} \\
M-theory is a generalization of $5$-superstring theories type I,IIA,IIB,SO(32),heterotic,E8xE8, while simplifies perturbation effect.
M-theory consists of $G_2$ holonomy in $11$-dimensional space. Joyce conjecture claims an extraction of symplectic structure from $G_2$,
by associative 3-form as an analogue of Calabi-Yau 3-fold. Typical strategy is deformation and wall-crossing formula.

\item \textbf{Anabelian Geometry} \\
Solution of quintic 1: Klein's icosahedral method and 2: p-adic henselization, and different Galois groups solving the same equation.
Grothendieck Teichmuller. This might be NT, but hoping to find connection with GIT.

\item \textbf{Abstract Algebra}\\
Graduate abstract algebra is typically commutative ring, matrix or Lie theory, not many associative algebra,
and I'll consider quantization and deformation for quiver algebra, Hecke algebra, Cherednik algebra etc.
Categorification of associative algebra might be interesting and more abstractly quiver $Rep(Q)$.
$Rep(Q)$ is abelian category but not symmetric monoidal, while $Rep(G)$ is.
Is there any categorification of Dynkin/non-Dynkin diagram? [link]

\item \textbf{Higgs Bundle} \\
Langlands related.
